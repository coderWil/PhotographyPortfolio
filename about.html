About Karina

I've spent the last decade photographing weddings across the country, and if there's one thing I've learned, it's that the best moments are rarely the posed ones. They're the shaky hands during vows, the best man forgetting his speech halfway through, the flower girl falling asleep at the reception. My job is to notice those moments before they pass and hold onto them so you can return to that day again and again.

My approach is quiet and unobtrusive — I'd rather blend into the background than direct your day. That means candid, documentary-style images alongside a handful of timeless portraits, all shot in natural light whenever possible. Above all, I want your photographs to feel like you: honest, warm, and entirely your own.

"Karina didn't just photograph our wedding — she captured how it felt to be there."

Elena & Marcus, Married 2025
